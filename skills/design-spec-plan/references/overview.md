## Spec planning in a minute

**The idea.** In spec-driven design, the written spec is the source of truth. It records how things should behave, so designers, engineers and AI tools build from the same page instead of guessing. Planning is where you decide how you'll write it, before you write any of it.

**The four skills**

| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|
| `design-spec-plan` | Optional, and you are here. Plans your spec set before you write it and saves `spec-plan.md` |
| `design-spec-authoring` | Writes and edits your specs; holds the shared rules |
| `design-spec-preview` | Shows your specs (with Figma, in `figma` mode) as a local, interactive visual check |
| `design-spec-audit` | Checks your specs without changing them, and keeps the audit register |

Use them in order, or jump in wherever your project is.

**What this skill does.** It asks a few questions about your project, or reads your notes, and recommends how to structure your specs. You get three things: a one-page plan card, a `spec-plan.md` file, and a prompt that starts authoring.
